About Us

About Rythu Mitra

Bridging the gap between Indian farmers and government welfare programs.

Millions of farmers across India miss out on crucial central and state agricultural subsidies, crop insurance benefits, and input assistance simply due to lack of accessible, localized information.

Rythu Mitra (meaning 'Farmer's Friend') was created to provide a simple, clean, and multilingual portal where any farmer—regardless of tech literacy—can discover all benefits they are entitled to in their native language.

Multilingual First

Full support for Telugu, Hindi, and English so farmers can comfortably navigate in their preferred tongue.

Accurate Land Filtering

Differentiates between small & marginal farmers (under 5 acres) and large holdings according to official norms.

Local & Offline-Ready

Runs completely in your browser with LocalStorage. Fast, zero tracking, and lightweight on rural internet.
